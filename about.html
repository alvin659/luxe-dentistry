About Luxe Dentistry

A family-owned dental clinic in Los Algodones, led by a Harvard-trained cosmetic and implant dentist with 25 years of experience.

Luxe Dentistry is a family-owned and operated clinic providing the full range of dental services in the heart of Los Algodones. It is led by Dr. Jose Manuel Jimenez, DDS, who has 25 years of experience, 15 of them right here, and who founded the clinic to bring world-class cosmetic and implant care to the patients who travel here from across North America.

Dr. Jimenez's true passion is cosmetic dentistry. He is recognized as one of the best dentists for smile makeovers in Los Algodones, having performed makeovers for hundreds of patients from across the United States, Canada and Mexico. His approach blends technical precision with an artist's eye, designing each smile to suit the individual person for a natural, healthy, confident result.

Education and training

  • Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS), University of Michoacana de San Nicolas de Hidalgo. Cosmetic Dentistry and Restorative Rehabilitation.
  • Implantology Diploma, Evidence-Based Implant Dentistry, Harvard School of Dental Medicine (Boston, USA campus).
  • Implant Certificate, Autonomous University of Baja California.
  • Member, Mexican Dental Association (ADM).

Professional experience

  • 2007 to 2018: Cosmetic Dentistry and Implantology Expert, Sani Dental Group, Los Algodones.
  • 2007: Dr. Chona Pua Dental Clinic, Salinas, California.
  • 1998 to 2006: La Ceyba Dental, Salinas, California.

The Harvard difference

Dr. Jimenez received the ultimate accomplishment as a diplomate of Harvard University for his continuing education in Evidence-Based Implant Dentistry, a mark of his commitment to placing implants using the most current, research-backed techniques.
